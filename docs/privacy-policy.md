# Dragon Go Client Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Dragon Go Client is an independent iOS app maintained by Niclas Eriksson. This policy covers the app and its push service. Contact: [dragongoclient@stackofreason.com](mailto:dragongoclient@stackofreason.com).

## Playing on Dragon Go Server

Your account and games are hosted by the separate service Dragon Go Server (DGS). Account registration opens DGS's website in your browser. The registration form goes directly to DGS; our push server does not receive that form or its full-name, email, or password fields.

The app sends your login details, moves, game settings, comments, and messages directly to DGS under its [policy](https://www.dragongoserver.net/policy.php). Messages reach their intended recipients; game visibility follows DGS's rules and your settings. Our push service separately handles the information described below.

The app keeps account details, login sessions, settings, and cached games on your device. Your DGS password is not sent to our push server.

## Push notifications

Our server receives your DGS account ID, username, login session and expiry, device notification token, app identifier, and registration and authentication records. It stores these alongside pending-game IDs, opponent usernames, and update times received from the app or DGS.

We use this information to check for turns and send alerts and badge counts. Stored DGS sessions are encrypted on our production server. We do not store full games or conversations on the push server.

Apple processes notification tokens and content, which can include opponent usernames and game counts. Previews may appear on your lock screen, depending on your settings.

## Hosting, security, and support

Fly.io hosts our push service and backups, primarily in Stockholm, Sweden. Services handling your connections receive your IP address. We temporarily use it for rate limiting and keep technical request and error logs for operations and security. Providers may process information in other countries.

Support emails and their contents are used to respond to you. Please never send passwords or session cookies.

The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell data or use it for advertising tracking. We share information to provide these services or as required by law.

## Retention and your choices

Game snapshots are updated as games change; queued alerts are removed after delivery or cancellation. Push credentials expire within 90 days, and DGS sessions are cleared when detected as expired or invalid. Some account, registration, and operational records have no fixed automatic deletion period. Backups may retain earlier copies.

Logging out requests removal of that device's registration and queued alerts, if the request reaches our server. It does not delete all server records or your DGS account. Disabling notifications in iOS does not delete server data.

Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of our records; we may verify account ownership. Contact DGS separately about its records.

Where applicable, you can also restrict or object to processing, request data portability, and complain to a data-protection authority. We process data to provide the service you request, for legitimate interests in security, operations, and support, and to meet legal obligations.

## Website and policy changes

Carrd hosts our website; its [privacy policy](https://carrd.com/docs/general/privacy) also applies. Other linked services have their own policies.

We update this page and its date when practices change, and announce material changes in the app or on the website.
