# AI use in Dragon Go Client

As with a lot of development these days, AI assistance is welcome! ✨
Here are a few expectations that apply equally to maintainers and contributors, across code, tests, documentation and discussions.

- **Understand your changes.** Review everything you submit and be able to explain
  what it does and why. You are responsible for it, whoever wrote it.
- **Verify the result.** Test the behavior, review generated tests, and say what
  you checked and what remains untested.
- **Keep it focused.** Submit changes that others can reasonably review and maintain.
- **Be open.** Disclose substantial AI assistance in the PR description or commit
  message. Explain changes and respond to feedback in your own words.
- **Respect trust.** Protect secrets and private data. Follow licensing and
  attribution requirements. Agents need explicit authorization to publish,
  deploy, or change account and security settings.

Ask for help when you are unsure 😊
We welcome learning. Contributions that leave the understanding and verification entirely to someone (or something) else will be declined.
